## Hey, I'm Josh 👋

I work with Robots and Systems to support Teaching and Research at the [University of Lincoln, School of Engineering & Physical Sciences](https://lincoln.ac.uk/socs) focusing in Computer Science and [L-CAS (Lincoln Centre for Autonomous Systems Research)](https://github.com/lcas) where I maintain container images to enable rapid prototyping such as for a student who is just getting started or a seasoned expert who just wants this out of their way.
