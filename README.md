## Hey, I'm Josh 👋

I work with robots and systems to support teaching and research at the [University of Lincoln, School of Engineering & Physical Sciences](https://lincoln.ac.uk/socs), focusing on computer science and [L-CAS (Lincoln Centre for Autonomous Systems Research)](https://github.com/lcas). I maintain container images that enable rapid prototyping, whether for a student who is just getting started or a seasoned expert who just wants the tooling out of the way.

I'm interested in getting systems into people's hands, whether that's high-quality teaching environments, reproducible research, or a product that provides a new experience.

You might also want to check out my website, [joshc.uk](https://joshc.uk/?ref=gh+readme), for more information about me and the things I do.
